⚠️ Market Inefficiency

The Problem: Fragmentation in Web3 Gaming

Current Web3 game developers face triple contract complexity, high gas costs, and fragmented user friction.

🏛️

Triple Contract Overhead

Games deploy separate ERC-20s for gold, ERC-1155s for consumables, and ERC-721s for equipment. This triples development, auditing, and maintenance costs.

⛽

High Gas & Multi-Approvals

Players must sign 3+ approval transactions to interact with game loops. Microtransactions become economically unviable due to cumulative network fees.

⚡

No Atomic Multi-Transfers

Transferring a loadout (500 gold + 3 potions + 1 sword) requires sequential calls across contracts, exposing trades to partial failures and front-running.
